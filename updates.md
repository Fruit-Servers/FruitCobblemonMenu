Welcome to Cobblemon Beta!
Welcome to Fruit Servers Cobblemon!

After months of planning and weeks of development, we're excited to finally open the doors and let you experience what we've been working on.

Cobblemon is a fresh new experience for Fruit Servers, built from the ground up with plenty of new features, ideas and adventures waiting for you to discover.

As this is a BETA, things will continue to change as we develop the server and listen to your feedback. If you find something that doesn't feel quite right, have an idea, or simply want to tell us what you're enjoying, we'd love to hear from you.

Most importantly, we hope you have fun, explore the world, catch some Pokémon and make some new memories with us.

Thank You, Beta Testers
A huge thank you to everyone who helped us test Cobblemon before launch. Your feedback, bug reports and time spent playing have helped us get the server ready for everyone.

We couldn't have done it without you!
